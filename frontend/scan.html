💡 Image Quality Best Practices

  • Adequate Lighting: Ensure high contrast and sharp focus on the region of clinical interest.
  • No Glare: If photographing an X-Ray viewbox or digital screen, avoid reflections and angle glare.
  • Unobstructed Margins: Include surrounding anatomical landmarks for proper spatial context.
  • Privacy Safety: Please crop out any personal identifiable identifiers (such as national ID or patient barcode) prior to scanning.
